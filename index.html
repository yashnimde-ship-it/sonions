VAULTBREAKERS

One saboteur. One mansion. Grab the loot and run.

How to play

Runners sneak into the mansion, grab loot and carry it to the getaway van. Loot slows you down, and getting hit makes you drop it.

The Saboteur sees the whole house and fires traps to stop them. Traps cost energy and need time to recharge.

After every round a new player becomes the Saboteur. Most points after the last round wins.

The safehouse

Everyone online meets in the safehouse. Walk into the 2, 3 or 4 player portal: when it fills up, your crew is sent off to its own heist. Change character at the wardrobe.

Controls

  • WASD or Arrows move, Shift sprint
  • E action: customize, open doors, steal
  • Esc back to the safehouse
  • Gamepad: stick move, RT sprint, A action
