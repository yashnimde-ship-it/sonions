How to play

Heist · 2 or more players

The Saboteur owns the house. During setup they hide the diamonds inside furniture (a fridge, a bed, a display case) and guard them with traps. Traps stay invisible until someone sets one off.

Runners search the furniture: hold E next to it. Carry each diamond you find to the GETAWAY. Bank enough before the 3-minute clock runs out and the Runners win; otherwise the Saboteur does. Get hit hard and you drop your diamond.

A heist match is a few rounds. Every round has a new Saboteur and a new map. You score for diamonds you bank, or as the Saboteur for traps that hit and diamonds left behind. Most points at the end wins.

Hide & Seek · 3 or more players

Choose Hide & Seek in the safehouse and join a crew of at least 3. One Seeker counts for 30 seconds while the Hiders find a place to hide on the cruise ship. Then the Seeker hunts with a flashlight and six shots; missed shots cost the Seeker health. Hiders caught become ghosts and watch the rest of the round.

Hiders win by surviving the hunt. The main entrance opens for the final 45 seconds: escape for a bonus. The Seeker can use Q for a flare and C to scan nearby hiding places; Hiders can use Q for a speed boost.

The safehouse

Everyone online meets in the safehouse. Walk into the 2 or 3 player portal, or the CUSTOM one for 4 to 9 players. When it fills, your crew starts the selected mode. Hide & Seek needs at least 3 players; smaller crews play Heist. Change character at the wardrobe; each crew member has Runner and Saboteur abilities.

Controls

  • WASD or Arrows move, Shift sprint
  • E action: open doors, grab a dropped diamond; hold it to search furniture
  • Q your character's ability for this round's role (a few uses per round)
  • Saboteur: click furniture to hide a diamond, 1-0 - = to pick a trap and click to place it, right-click to take back; during the live round, right-click an unused trap and click open floor to reposition it once. WASD and the mouse wheel move the camera
  • Hide & Seek: the Seeker uses Click to shoot, R to reload, Q for Flare, C to scan; Hiders use E to hide and Q to boost
  • Esc leave the match, back to the safehouse
  • Gamepad: stick move, RT sprint, A action
